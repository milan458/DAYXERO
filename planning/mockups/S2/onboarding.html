S2.12 App Store review prompt. My recommendation is to not ask during onboarding, since they haven't used the program yet. Ask after they complete Day 1 or their first ridden-out craving, using Apple's built-in prompt (it limits itself to three times a year). Your call.

S2.13 Analytics and skips. Every screen sends a "step viewed" event and every Skip sends "step skipped", so PostHog shows the drop-off per screen. Optional steps (triggers, reasons, past attempts, NRT) all have Skip; required ones (age, products, baseline, Quit Day) don't.

Accessibility. All screens scroll at the largest text sizes with the button pinned to the bottom; the stepper supports VoiceOver swipe up/down; every option card reads as one element with its selected state.
