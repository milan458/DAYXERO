Description (draft)

DayXero is a quit program for nicotine: cigarettes, vapes and nicotine pouches, alone or together.

A plan, not just a counter. A few days of prep, then Quit Day, a quit week with a short lesson each day, and four weeks of weekly check-ins to make it stick.

Craving SOS, from anywhere. Three minutes of guided breathing and an urge-surfing timer, with your own reasons and photos. Start it from the app, a widget, Control Center or Siri, and follow it on the Lock Screen.

Slips never reset your progress. Log one, talk it through in a minute, and leave with a plan for next time. Your days clear stay as they are.

See your patterns. When cravings hit, what sets them off, and how this week compares with the last.

Works with your NRT. Keep your patch, gum or lozenge plan on track with reminders that follow the label on your pack. Pouch users can step down their strength first.

Private by design. No account. Your plan, reasons and logs stay on your iPhone.

DayXero offers education and support. It isn't medical treatment and doesn't replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist.

Subscription: start with Prep Day 1 and Craving SOS for free. DayXero Pro unlocks the full program: annual (with a 3-day free trial), monthly or lifetime. Payment is charged to your Apple ID; subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Terms: Apple's standard EULA. Privacy: [your privacy policy URL].
