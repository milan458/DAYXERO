Bio-Orb in the app

The Bio-Orb replaces the flat breathing circle on the Craving SOS session (S6) and the Longer out-breath tool (S12). Every phone below is live: it runs the app's own breathing timing (4 s in, 6 s out, slowing toward 5 breaths a minute), so dark and light move together. Each screen is shown in both themes.

CoreA glassy sage sphere. It grows on the in-breath and shrinks on the out-breath, eased with the same cosine curve as CravingWave.breath.
RingsThree sage rings follow the core with increasing lag (breath damping), so the outer ring is the last to arrive. That trailing edge is what makes it feel like breath, not a pulse.
Speech energyWhen a coaching line plays, the voice's loudness ripples the rings. Silence leaves a faint resting shimmer.
ThemeDark: the orb glows out of black. Light: a paler core with a darker sage rim (accent ink #55621F), so it doesn't wash out on #F3F4EE.
