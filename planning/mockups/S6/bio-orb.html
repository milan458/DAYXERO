Bio-Orb in the app

Your Bio-Orb (the glowing sage ring) placed on the app's breathing screens: Craving SOS (S6) and the calm tools (S12). Each screen is shown in dark and light, and all of them are live. The ring swells on the in-breath, holds, and draws in on the out-breath. While a coaching line plays, the voice makes it ripple. The ring sits straight on the screen background, with no dish behind it.
