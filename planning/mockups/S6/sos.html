Section 6 · mockups for sign-off

Craving SOS

One tap from anywhere opens straight into the session: no loading, no questions first. About three minutes of guided breathing and an urge-surfing timer, with your own reasons and photo. Then you say how it went. Craving SOS is free for everyone. Example: Milan, vapes and smokes, Day 3.

Not shown as screens

Haptics (S6.2). A soft pulse follows the breathing: a gentle rise on the in-breath, a slow fade on the out-breath. It starts at about 6 breaths a minute and slows towards 5 by the end. Haptics follow the system setting, and there's an on/off switch in the session's ••• menu.

Reduce Motion. The circle stays still and only the words change ("Breathe in… hold… out"), with a thin progress ring. VoiceOver reads the cue each time it changes.

Logging (S6.6). Every session is saved with its start time, length, outcome (passed, slipped or closed) and, if given, a trigger and strength. Insights (Section 7) use these.

Closing early. The ✕ ends the session without asking anything; it's saved as "closed" so the timer and Live Activity stop.

Speed. The session screen draws nothing from the network and opens before anything else loads, so it appears in under a second.
