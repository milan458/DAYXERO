Milestones and share cards

A moment to mark each milestone, and a card to share it if you want to. Sharing is always opt-in: the card shows only the days by default, you choose anything else, and there's never a name or photo unless you add it. Every card carries the DayXero wordmark. Example: Milan reaching one week.

Not shown as screens

When it appears. The first time the app opens on or after a milestone day, before Home. If several were missed (say after a break), only the latest is celebrated; the others are filled in on the milestones row.

Slips. Milestones follow days clear, so a slip never takes one away.

Reduce Motion. The rings and sparks don't move; the number fades in. The success haptic still plays unless haptics are off.

How cards are made. Drawn on the phone (SwiftUI ImageRenderer) at full Instagram Story size (1080 × 1920) or square (1080 × 1080) for WhatsApp and others. Nothing is uploaded.
