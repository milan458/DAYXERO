Slip handling

A slip never resets anything and never shames. You can log one from Craving SOS, the Log sheet or the widget. Then there's an optional one-minute talk-through: what happened, what set it off, and one thing to try next time, saved as an if-then plan that Craving SOS shows you later. All supportive wording is placeholder until your copy arrives. Example: Milan, Day 12.

Not shown as screens

Nothing resets. Days clear, the Home hero, the week strip, money kept and milestones are all unchanged by a slip. The only places a slip appears are the Progress tab's patterns and recent logs.

Skippable at every step. "Not now" and "Skip" are always there. A slip is saved the moment it's logged; the talk-through only adds details.

No streak language. No "you broke your streak", no counters going back to zero, no red. The error haptic is never used for slips; a soft one is.
