Daily pledge and evening check-in

Two small daily moments. In the morning, a pledge made with one of your own reasons and its photo. In the evening, a one-minute check-in: how today went, cravings, mood. Both are optional, never nag, and neither changes days clear. A day ends when you say it does, so a 1am check-in counts for the day you lived. Example: Milan on Day 13.

Not shown as screens

Which reason. The pledge shows a different reason each day, in turn, and prefers ones with a photo. People with no reasons yet are asked to add one the first time.

Missed a pledge? Nothing happens. The pledge card stays until midday, then quietly goes. In the evening the check-in card appears instead.

"Had a slip" in the check-in. If no slip was logged today, it offers to log one and opens the slip talk-through (S8). If one was logged, it just says so. Days clear never change.

Accessibility. Press-and-hold has a double-tap alternative for VoiceOver and Switch Control, and Reduce Motion fills the ring without animation.
