Crew speech bubbles

Five screens where Xero, Jelli and Glim talk in bubbles that arrive one after another. Each phone shows the finished screen; Replay plays the arrival. Hover or tab through a line in a table to find its bubble.

Lines the app already uses (approved in v4 or since) are kept word for word and only move into a bubble. Only the lines marked New need your yes.

Approved, reusedmoves into a bubble, same wordsNewneeds approval

How every bubble behaves

  • ShapeRadius 18 with one 4 pt corner pointing at whoever speaks. Cream on colour grounds; white with a hairline on the reading ground.
  • ArrivalTyping dots for 0.4 s, then the line rises in (Arrive motion). The next bubble follows 1.2 s later. Earlier bubbles stay.
  • LimitThree bubbles at most per character on a screen. A bubble never holds a button, and nothing waits for the bubbles to finish except where noted.
  • Reduce MotionAll bubbles show at once, no dots, no rise.
  • VoiceOverThe bubbles read as one passage in order, starting with who is speaking ("Xero says…"). A large first bubble keeps its header trait.
  • Large textBubbles over art cap at xxxLarge, as Welcome's bubble does today; the others grow and the screen scrolls.
