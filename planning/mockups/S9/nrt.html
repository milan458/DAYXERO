NRT and taper co-pilot

Helps people stick to the nicotine replacement plan printed on their own pack, and gives pouch users a strength taper. DayXero never chooses a dose: you copy your pack's schedule (we offer the common label versions to start from), and every screen says to follow the label and ask a pharmacist or doctor. Example: Milan, 12 cigarettes a day, starting a patch on Quit Day.

Not shown as screens

Where it starts. Home shows "Set up NRT" for people who said in onboarding they use NRT or would like to. It's also reachable from the slip talk-through's "Look at NRT" (S8) and from Settings (S15).

Labels differ. US patch labels come in a 10-week version (21 mg for 6 weeks, 14 mg for 2, 7 mg for 2) and an 8-week version (4, 2, 2 weeks). Gum and lozenge labels use a 12-week schedule (one piece every 1–2 hours in weeks 1–6, every 2–4 hours in weeks 7–9, every 4–8 hours in weeks 10–12). The app only offers these as starting points to match against the pack, each with its source, and lets you edit every step.

No advice beyond the label. No "you should use more", no adjusting doses for slips or cravings, no combination therapy suggestions. If someone asks for more help, the answer is always a pharmacist or doctor.

Stopping or pausing. NRT can be paused or removed in Settings at any time; reminders stop with it.
