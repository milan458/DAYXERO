Progress, insights and logging

The third tab in the dock. The top half is what you've gained; the bottom half is your patterns, once there's a week of data. Logging is one tap, from the app, a widget or Siri, with details only if you want to add them. Slips show up here and nowhere else. Example: Milan on Day 12, vapes (main) and smokes, Quit Day Mon 12 Oct.

Not shown as screens

What counts as "a week of data". Insights open once your first log is at least 7 days old and there are at least 5 logs (cravings, SOS sessions, uses or slips). Until then the section shows how far along you are.

Prep logging feeds the plan. Uses logged in prep are tracking, not slips, and they fill the heat map so the quit week starts with real patterns.

Charts and accessibility. Each chart has a one-line summary above it that VoiceOver reads first ("Most cravings: weekday mornings"), and the heat map can be explored cell by cell. Largest text sizes stack the tiles in one column.

Editing logs. Recent logs are listed under the charts; swipe to delete or tap to add details. Nothing here changes days clear.
