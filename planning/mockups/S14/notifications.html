Section 14 · mockups for sign-off

Notifications

One plan for every notification DayXero sends, each with its own switch, plus quiet hours. The rules: never guilt, never "streak" talk, never more than three a day from the program (NRT reminders you set yourself don't count), and nothing once something's already done. After a few quiet days we send two gentle notes and then stop, following your pause-and-replay rule.

The plan

Morning pledge 8:00, skipped once pledged · Lesson ready 12:00 on days a new lesson opens, skipped once started · Evening check-in 21:00, skipped once checked in · Milestones 9:00 on the day · NRT at the times you set in Section 9 · Away two notes after 2 and 3 quiet days, then nothing · Trial ending the day before, from Section 3.

Daily cap. At most three program notifications a day. If more are due, the order is milestone, pledge, lesson, check-in; the lowest drops. NRT and trial reminders are always sent.

Quiet hours (default 22:30 to 7:30). Anything due inside them waits until they end, except times you've set yourself.

Words we never use in notifications: streak, broke, missed, lose, failed, "don't forget", "you haven't". Every notification is checked against this list by a test.
