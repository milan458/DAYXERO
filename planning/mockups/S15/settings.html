Settings, privacy and accessibility

Everything you can change, in one plain list behind the profile button on Home. Your data stays on your iPhone, you can export it or delete it all, and the privacy page says exactly what anonymous usage data is sent. Plus the accessibility pass across the whole app and the health disclaimer.

Accessibility pass (S15.5), not shown as screens

VoiceOver. Every control gets a label and hint; the Home hero reads as one sentence ("12 days clear, two weeks in 2 days"); charts read their one-line summary first. A full VoiceOver run on a real iPhone through every section, including Sections 1–3.

Dynamic Type. Every screen checked at the largest accessibility size; layouts stack instead of truncating (screenshots already cover this per section).

Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency. Motion falls back to fades everywhere; with Reduce Transparency the glass cards and dock become solid surfaces.

Contrast. Text and controls checked against WCAG AA in both themes; the light theme's sage text already uses the darker ink (#55621F) for this.
