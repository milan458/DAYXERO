## Substitutions (things the app can't show yet)

1. **Reveal timeline chips** (cards 1 and 3). A reveal card has no chip field, so the chips (when · title, quoted from `health-timeline.json`) go on the last lines of `answer`, after a blank line. On card 2, the bold chip inside "Go deeper" becomes plain text: "for most people, 1 to 2 weeks · Mood changes from withdrawal usually ease."
2. **Body text on a checklist or reflect.** `P7.4b` and `P7.4c` carry the draft's body line in a `body` field, but `ChecklistBeat` and `ReflectBeat` don't render `body` yet. They need to show it under the title, or the line could be folded into the title.
3. **Body text on a choice.** For `P7.4d` and `P7.4e`, the draft's body line is in `footer`, which `ChoiceBeat` shows under the options.
4. **Reflect buttons.** The draft asks for "Save my note" with a quiet "Skip for now". `ReflectBeat` shows Continue, and Skip because the beat is `optional: true`. The helper chips are `chips` (tap to insert).
5. **`prefillFrom: morning_anchor` on a choice** (`P7.4d`). `ChoiceBeat` only restores from its own `saveAs`, so it ignores `prefillFrom`. The app needs to preselect the option whose text matches `morning_anchor`.
6. **"Then I'll…" options that depend on screen 4** (`P7.4e`). A choice can't switch its options on an earlier answer, and it can't put `morning_wait_plan` first. I used one combined list of the per-trigger suggestions (water, shower, outside, walk, somewhere new, phone down, gum, text a friend, Craving SOS), plus `allowOther`. To build it as written, the app needs options keyed by `dx_first_trigger`, and `morning_wait_plan` offered first when P2 saved one.
7. **Plan card "Tomorrow, if it's {dx_first_trigger}, then I'll {dx_first_hour_plan}" plus the note** (`P7.4f`). I used `plan_card` with `fields: [dx_first_trigger, dx_first_hour_plan, note_to_dayxero]`. `PlanCardBeat.heading` has no entries for these keys, so it would show "Dx First Trigger" and so on. It needs headings: `dx_first_trigger` → "Tomorrow, if it's", `dx_first_hour_plan` → "Then I'll", `note_to_dayxero` → "Your note for the morning". It also needs the `situation` phrasing from the draft's build notes. Rows that are empty already hide.
8. **The Prep 6 plan card** (`P7.4g`). This is the same `plan_card` as P6.5g (`trigger_plans`, `support_person`, `urge_signals`, `editable`). It doesn't collapse, and it isn't hidden when `trigger_plans` is empty; only the empty rows hide. The line "You made these on Prep 6. They're ready for tomorrow." moves into the closing info.
9. **Closing screen and completion message** (`P7.4h`). The Gollwitzer line, the source and the completion message ("Your space is clearer and your morning has a plan. All that's left today is the last one, this evening.") are one `info` beat. The source goes on the body's last line, because an info beat has no source field.
10. **After the evening hold** (`P7.5b`, `P7.5c`). "Ready. Your note will be waiting in the morning." is an `info` beat after the commit. "Still to clear tonight" is a second checklist with the same items and the same `saveAs: clearout_checklist`. `ChecklistBeat` restores the earlier ticks from that key, so items ticked in step 4 show as done and the rest can be ticked now. Unlike the draft, the whole list shows, and it still shows when everything is already ticked. To match the draft, it should list only the unticked items and skip itself when there are none.
11. **"Opens from 6 pm"** for P7.5 isn't in the schema. The evening card relies on `ground: violet`, and the 6 pm gate needs app logic.
12. **The hold's `holdSeconds: 3`** is kept as the draft and old beat say. `CommitBeat` now uses `DX.Move.hold` for every hold, and the plain hold says "Hold for 1 second", while the P7.5 script says "press and hold … for three seconds". One of them needs to change.
