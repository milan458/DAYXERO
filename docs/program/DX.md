### Script (Eleven v4)

Voice direction: Warm, grounded and quietly confident, a calm morning voice with a little lift in it; no hype, no solemnity. About 130 words a minute, slowing for the breaths at the end.

## Chapter 1 · Good morning (0:00)

[warmly] Good morning. If you wrote a note last night, you've just read it. [pause] Take a second with it. That was you, looking out for you.

Today is Day Xero. [pause] And before anything else, I'd like you to hear this. The hardest part is already behind you. For seven days you've looked at nicotine with your eyes open. You've seen the gap, the autopilot, the stories. You've seen that the relief was mostly the gap closing, and that the gap was made by the last dose. [pause]

So today you're not losing anything. You're simply not refilling it. You're not someone who can't {s: smoke | v: vape | p: use pouches} today. You're someone who doesn't need to. [pause]

Maybe you woke up feeling calm, even a little excited. Maybe you woke up nervous, already bracing for the first craving. Both are completely normal. How you feel this morning doesn't decide how today goes. [pause]

## Chapter 2 · What today might hold (1:10)

Let's be honest about today, so nothing catches you off guard.

At some point in the next few hours, your body will notice the nicotine isn't coming. That's usually when the first cravings show up. You might feel restless, a bit irritable, or find it harder to focus. You might be hungrier than usual. [pause] Those are normal signs of your body adjusting. They're usually strongest in the first days, and they ease. None of it means something's going wrong. It means something has started. [pause]

Here's a way to see it. The gap you've watched all week is going to open today, and this time nothing will fill it. That's most of the discomfort: the same gap you already know, just left open. And the longer it's left open, the more your body gets used to living without it. [pause]

And quietly, in the background, other things are starting too. {s: Within minutes of stopping, your heart rate and blood pressure start to come down. And over the next few days, the carbon monoxide in your blood drops back to normal. | v: Nicotine itself clears from your blood within hours, and within a few days most of what's left is gone. | p: Nicotine itself clears from your blood within hours, and within a few days most of what's left is gone.} You'll see more of that in the next step.

So take today in small pieces. Not forever. Just this morning. Then lunch. Then the afternoon.

## Chapter 3 · Your first craving (2:45)

Now, the first craving. Let's plan for it, so it doesn't surprise you. [pause]

It might come with your first coffee, after breakfast, in the car, or the first time you sit down to work. Last night you made a plan for the first one. When it comes, use it.

And then, this. Notice it. [pause] Name it: "There's a craving." [pause] Find where it sits in your body. Chest, hands, mouth, stomach. [pause] Let it be there. You don't have to fight it, and you don't have to do what it says. [pause] Breathe slowly, a little longer out than in. And watch it like a wave. It rises, it peaks, and it falls, usually within a few minutes. [pause]

If you'd like company while it passes, open Craving SOS. It's always in the bar at the bottom of the app, and you'll try a practice run in a few minutes, so you know exactly what to expect. When the wave has passed, tap "It passed." [pause] Every one you ride teaches your brain that it can.

## Chapter 4 · If it gets hard (4:15)

Some moments today might be tough. That's not a sign you're doing it wrong. It's what a first day can feel like. [pause] So, a few things to keep close.

If you're using nicotine replacement, use it the way your pack says. It takes the edge off the physical side, and it isn't cheating. Not using it is fine too.

Tell someone that today's the day. A message back from a friend can carry you through a wave.

Eat proper meals, keep water close, and if you drink coffee, {s: have about half your usual. Now that you've stopped smoking, the same cup hits harder. | v: notice whether it leaves you jittery. That can feel a lot like a craving. | p: notice whether it leaves you jittery. That can feel a lot like a craving.}

And if you slip, [pause] it doesn't wipe anything out. Your count isn't reset. Tap "I slipped," be kind to yourself, notice what set it off, and carry on from right there. A slip is information, not the end of anything. The very next choice is the one that matters.

## Chapter 5 · Starting (5:25)

[calmly] So, here you are. [pause] Take a slow breath in. [pause] And a long breath out. [long pause]

Think of your reason. The one that matters most. [long pause]

Now picture today going the way you'd like it to. Not perfectly. Just one wave at a time, each one passing. [long pause]

When you're ready, press and hold the button on your screen for three seconds. When you let go, your counter starts, and Day Xero begins. [pause]

You're not losing anything today. You're getting through one day, one wave at a time. [pause] I'll be with you this evening.
