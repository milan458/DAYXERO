What's changing · Vaping

Healthier lips and gums

2

Starts in 2 days

Usually 2 to 6 weeks after Quit Day

Nicotine tightens the small blood vessels in your gums. That can hide bleeding and slow healing.

As blood flow comes back, your gums get pinker and firmer and your lips less dry.

What you might notice

Pinker, firmer gums

Fresher breath and a sharper sense of taste

A little bleeding when you brush at first. That's healing, not harm.

Source: NHS Better Health
