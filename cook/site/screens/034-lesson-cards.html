You're the driver

Think of your quit as a long drive. You're at the wheel, and you choose where the car goes.

Cravings are the noisy passengers. They shout, they bargain, they promise that one won't hurt.

You don't have to argue or throw them out. Let them talk. Keep your eyes on the road.
