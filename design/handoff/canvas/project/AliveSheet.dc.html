Every screen owns a full colour. Dark mode keeps the hue and deepens it, so the app stays colourful at night.

2 · Nothing sits still
Motion has five jobs. Each has one timing so the whole app feels like one hand made it.
Arrive · content rises 18px and fades in, 90ms apart, 600ms ease-out. Hero art first, then title, then cards, then the button.
Breathe · 4s in, 6s out. Characters, circles and words all share this clock in SOS and intros.
Ambient · clouds drift 20–26s, waves roll 5–8s, stars and fireflies twinkle 1.5–3s. Always slow.
Count · numbers that matter count up over 0.8s: days, waves, money, sky %.
Hand over · when one character passes you to another, the first waves and slides out, the next slides in.
Reduce Motion on · everything appears at its final state. Breathing switches to a gentle colour fade. Haptics carry the rhythm.
3 · It's about you
Everything onboarding learns shows up again. Signal in, what changes:
Time of day → who greets you (Xero or Glim), the screen colour, the scene and Today's top card
Days clear → sky clarity, character growth stage, which lesson unlocks
Product → units everywhere: cigarettes, puffs or pouches not used
Triggers + logged cravings → "your coffee wave usually comes around 9:40" and Jelli waiting just before it
Your reason + photo → pledge, SOS and milestone cards quote it back
Today's mood + cravings → Glim's evening line reflects your actual day
Your plan (if-then from a slip) → resurfaces as a tip the next time that trigger is near
Try it: select the Today screen and open Tweaks. Change time, product, trigger, day and name and watch it rebuild itself.
