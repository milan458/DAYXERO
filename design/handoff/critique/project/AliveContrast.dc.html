The blues, violets and green stay rich at low light. Apricot (#2B1A10) and sun (#2E2508) become brown and olive, and coral (#2A1020) becomes maroon. That's how colour behaves near black, not a bad pick. Brown grounds will read as dirty next to the navy-family screens.
Option A: keep the warm grounds and test them on an OLED phone at low brightness.
Option B (my pick): the three warm screens use a deep plum ground in dark mode and keep their colour in the hero art and the button.
