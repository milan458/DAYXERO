The screens are good one by one. As a set, nothing is held to a rule.

FIX NOWColour
18 screens, one ground. Dark mode: 18 screens, one navy.
Every Crew screen sits on #FBF7F1, and every dark screen on #0E0B2B. Only the hero art tells SOS from Progress. Once you scroll past the art, Today, Program, Progress and Slip are hard to tell apart. This is the drift you asked about.
Fix: carry Alive's rule over. One owned colour per screen family (Xero apricot, Jelli ocean, Glim violet), with cream only for dense reading screens (Program, Lesson, Progress).

FIX NOWStory logic
“The sky clears as you do” is broken by the numbers.
