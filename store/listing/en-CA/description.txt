Quit smoking, vaping or nicotine pouches without going cold turkey. DayXero gives you 7 days to get ready, then you quit on your Day Xero, with a small crew at your side the whole way.

PREP FOR 7 DAYS, THEN QUIT
Keep using as normal while you learn how your habit really works. Each prep day is a short hand of cards: listen, reveal, myth or fact, try. On Day Xero you're ready, not just hoping.

MEET THE CREW
Xero walks you through each day and shows you just the next step. Jelli breathes with you when a craving hits. Glim checks in each evening and helps you wind down.

CRAVING SOS, ANY TIME
Tap SOS and ride the wave out with Jelli, one breath at a time, with your own reasons in front of you. Start it from the app, a widget or Siri, and follow it on the Lock Screen.

SLIPS DON'T RESET YOUR PROGRESS
Tell Jelli what happened, keep your days, and make a one-minute plan for next time.

CALM TOOLS FOR EVERY WAVE
Box breathing, grounding, noticing the urge and a wind-down breath before bed.

PROGRESS, NOT PERFECTION
Badges as your days add up, every wave you ride out counted, and a health timeline with its sources.

WORKS WITH YOUR NRT
Using patches, gum or lozenges? Keep your plan on track with reminders that follow the label on your own pack.

PRIVATE ON YOUR PHONE
No account. Your plan, reasons and logs stay on your iPhone.
