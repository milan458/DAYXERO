Questions, answered.

When can I download DayXero?

DayXero is in development and not on the App Store yet. Join the beta list and we’ll email you a TestFlight invite as spots open, then the App Store link on launch day.

Why not just quit today?

Quitting on impulse is hard. DayXero gives you seven days of prep while you still use as normal, so you learn your habit and practise riding cravings before your Quit Day.

What happens if I slip?

Nothing resets. Your days clear and milestones stay as they are. You tell Jelli what happened, make one small plan for next time, and carry on.

Does it work for vapes and nicotine pouches?

Yes. DayXero is built for cigarettes, vapes and nicotine pouches, including people who use more than one.

Can I use it with patches or gum?

Yes. The NRT plan follows the step-down schedule of patches, gum and lozenges, with reminders. Follow the product instructions or your doctor’s advice on dosing.

Will DayXero be free?

The counter, Craving SOS, the whole prep week and Day Xero are free. The full program is planned at $9.99 a month, $39.99 a year with a 3-day free trial that starts on your Quit Day, or $79.99 once. Apple handles payments and cancellation.

What is the founding-member offer?

The first 500 people who join and confirm their email get the Annual plan at half price for their first year. We’ll send a personal App Store offer code at launch. You don’t pay anything now.

What will you do with my email?

Send a confirmation, your TestFlight invite, the launch link and rare beta updates. We don’t sell or share it, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
