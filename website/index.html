FAQ

Questions, Answered

What is DayXero?

DayXero is an iPhone app that helps you quit vaping, smoking and nicotine pouches. Instead of asking you to quit today, it guides you through a five-day prep phase, then supports you on your Quit Day and through the weeks after.

How is DayXero different from a quit smoking tracker?

Counter apps only track days. DayXero prepares you before your Quit Day, uses ACT urge surfing to get you through cravings in three minutes, and treats a slip as information instead of resetting your streak.

Does DayXero work for vapes and nicotine pouches?

Yes. DayXero supports cigarettes, vapes and nicotine pouches, including step-down plans that lower pouch strength and count over time.

Can I use DayXero with nicotine patches or gum?

Yes. DayXero's NRT co-pilot follows the step-down schedule of nicotine replacement products such as patches, gum and lozenges, with reminders. Follow the product instructions or your doctor's advice on dosing.

Is DayXero free?

The free version includes the core counter, the Craving SOS urge surfer and Day 1 of the prep program. The full program is $9.99 a month, $39.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, or $79.99 once for lifetime access.

Can I cancel my DayXero subscription easily?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so you can cancel in two taps from your iPhone settings, with no hidden fees and no ads.
